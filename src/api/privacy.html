TRUST & TRANSPARENCY

Privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026 · Applies to this Northstar application

This policy describes the current application. Your clinic or hosting organization determines how its deployment handles patient information, retention, and permitted access. Ask that organization for its applicable patient privacy notice.

Information the application handles

Northstar can process clinical records, patient identifiers, appointments, messages, intake forms, and integration traffic supplied by an authorized organization. It also stores administrator account details, password hashes, API-key hashes, configuration, and access audit records. Which clinical information is present depends on the deployment and its connected systems.

Why information is used

Information supports the workflows configured by the organization: connecting systems, preparing visits, displaying authorized records, routing work, and maintaining access and delivery history. This policy does not authorize a new use of patient information.

Storage and access

This local deployment stores application data in a SQLite database on its host computer. Passwords and API keys are stored as hashes. Access is controlled by credentials, scopes, tenant boundaries, and applicable patient directives. Encryption of the host volume, backups, retention settings, and external integrations require deployment-specific configuration.

Cookies and browser storage

Administrator sign-in uses an essential, HttpOnly session cookie that expires after eight hours or on sign-out. The workspace may remember an API key you explicitly enter in browser storage for legacy API-key access. Language preferences and patient portal sessions may also use browser storage or essential cookies. Do not use a shared browser for access credentials.

Sharing and integrations

Configured channels, identity providers, notification services, and other integrations may receive information needed for their configured purpose. The operating organization is responsible for authorizing recipients and reviewing their arrangements. Contacting us through the demo buttons sends email through your chosen email service to our team.

Retention and your requests

Clinical data and audit records follow the operating organization’s retention rules and the application’s configured retention controls. Contact your clinic or data custodian for requests to access, correct, or otherwise exercise rights concerning your records. We do not promise that clinical or audit data can be deleted on request where retention duties apply.

Contact and updates

For questions about this application’s privacy notice, use the Email our privacy team button. Do not include patient records or credentials. We will update this page when the application’s handling changes; the date above identifies this version.
